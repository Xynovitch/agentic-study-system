# Week 1 Advanced Synthesis Essay — Why Computers Use Binary (이진법)

Computers use binary (이진법) because transistors (트랜지스터) can only be on or off, so
naturally everything must be two states. Since there are two states, this is why binary is the
best system, and therefore it is also why computers are fast: fewer states means less to check.

Encoding (인코딩) text into binary is simple — you just assign each letter a number and convert
the number to binary, which proves that any information at all can be stored perfectly with no
loss. Because ASCII (아스키) uses 8 bits, it can represent every character in every language,
which is why we never need anything else.

Finally, since binary is base-2 and humans use base-10, binary is harder for humans but easier
for machines, so the conversion between them is the only real difficulty in computing.
